FAQ

Questions About Air Purifier for Smoke, Cooking Odor and Household Smells

Is activated carbon important for smoke?

Yes. Activated carbon is commonly used for odor-focused filtration, while HEPA-focused media addresses particles.

Can I use a small purifier for smoke in a large room?

A purifier should match the room size. For large or open spaces, choose a model with stronger airflow and coverage.

How often should filters be changed for smoke use?

Smoke and odor-heavy environments may require more frequent filter checks and replacement than light-use bedrooms.
