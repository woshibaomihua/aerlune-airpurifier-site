Questions About Air Purifier for Pets, Dander and Everyday Odor

Can an air purifier remove pet odor?

Activated carbon can help reduce everyday household odors, while HEPA-focused filtration supports particle capture such as dander and dust.

Should I run a pet air purifier all day?

For active pet homes, continuous or auto-mode operation can help maintain steadier air cleaning, depending on room conditions and filter life.
