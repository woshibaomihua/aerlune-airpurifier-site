Questions About Large Room Air Purifier for Living Rooms and Open Spaces

What is the best air purifier for a large living room?

Choose a model with enough room coverage and CADR for the space. AERLUNE Pro 600 is positioned for large rooms and open areas.

Can one purifier clean an open-plan space?

It depends on size, layout and airflow. For open spaces, choose stronger coverage and avoid blocking air intake or outlet.

Does CADR matter for large rooms?

Yes. CADR helps shoppers compare airflow and cleaning capacity across models.
