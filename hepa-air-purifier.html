What HEPA means for shoppers

A HEPA air purifier guide gives Google and buyers a clear educational page. It should explain particle-focused filtration, why carbon filters are different and how room size affects performance.

HEPA vs activated carbon

HEPA-focused media is used for particle capture, while activated carbon is commonly used for odor-focused filtration. Premium product pages should explain both without overclaiming.

No. HEPA-focused media and activated carbon serve different roles in a filter system.

What does CADR mean?

CADR is a common way to compare air delivery performance across air purifiers.
