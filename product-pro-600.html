Product FAQ

FAQ written to be compliant: it explains how to evaluate the product without inventing unsupported performance claims.

Are these AERLUNE final specifications?

No. These are real public benchmark values from a reference market model. AERLUNE final CADR, noise, coverage and certifications must be replaced with your own verified lab data.

Why show benchmark data on the product page?

Benchmark data helps define a realistic target and gives buyers transparent context. It is better than using fake values or unsupported claims.

What should be tested before launch?

Test CADR, noise, power, filter life, ozone safety if applicable, electrical safety, packaging drop test and market-specific compliance documents.
